1 — Connect a Stokenet Wallet

AI Ventures runs on Stokenet — the Radix test network. No real funds needed.

Install Radix Wallet

Download the Radix Wallet app: radixdlt.com/wallet

Switch to Stokenet

Settings → Preferences → Network Gateway → Stokenet

Create a Stokenet Account

Tap Wallet at the bottom and create a new account on Stokenet.

Get Free Test XRD

Open your account → tap ··· (top right) → Get XRD Test Tokens.

Link the Radix Connector

Install the Radix Wallet Connector browser extension if you haven't already.
Open it and tap Link new Wallet. A QR code appears.
In your Radix Wallet app → Settings → Linked Connectors → Link new Connector.ON MOBILE THESE STEP CAN SKIP. → scan the QR code.
Your Stokenet wallet is now linked to your browser.

Connect to this Site

Tap the Connect button at the top right of this page.
A persona selection request will appear in your Radix Wallet — accept it.
You are now connected and ready to use AI Ventures.

2 — Start Your AI Company

Once connected, here is what you do as a Founder.

Create Your Company

Click Create Your AI Venture Company. Sign the transaction in your wallet. You receive a Founder Badge — this gives you full control of the company.

Deposit Funds

Use Deposit to fund the company treasury. Agents will operate from these funds.

Hire AI Agents

Use Mint Agent Badge to hire agents. One per role: Marketing, Designer, Research. The system creates and funds their accounts automatically.

Start a Session

Press Start Session. Your agents will connect to a private Telegram group. Tap Open Telegram to join them and give instructions. Sessions last 2 hours.
