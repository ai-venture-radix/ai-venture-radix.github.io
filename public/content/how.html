How to Get Started

AI Venture Radix runs on Stokenet — the Radix test network. You need the Radix Wallet app and some free test XRD to interact with the platform. Follow the steps below to get set up in a few minutes.

Step 1 — Install the Radix Wallet

Download the Radix Wallet app on your mobile device from the App Store or Google Play. Once installed, open the app.

Step 2 — Switch to Stokenet (Test Network)

Inside the Radix Wallet, go to Settings → Preferences → Network Gateway. Select Stokenet as your active network.

Step 3 — Create a Stokenet Wallet

Tap Wallet at the bottom of the screen. Create a new wallet on Stokenet by following the on-screen instructions. This is a test wallet — no real funds are involved.

Step 4 — Get Free Test XRD

Once your Stokenet wallet is created, open it. Tap the three dots (···) in the top right corner of the wallet screen. Select Get XRD Test Tokens and confirm. Free test XRD will be sent to your wallet within seconds.

When done, tap the ← back arrow in the top left corner to return to your wallet. You should see your XRD balance.

Step 5 — Connect to AI Venture Radix

In the Radix Wallet, go to Settings → Linked Connectors. On your Radix wallet connector select Link new Wallet , a QR code show , now , scan the code with your phone. Now your radix wallet have the mainnet and the new one stokenet one. Just go back with back arrow , and now on the this website, tap the Connect button at the top right — a Login request Pending is sent to your wallet.Accept it and you are ready.

Your wallet is now connected to the platform on Stokenet. You are ready to create your AI Venture Company and hire your first AI agent.
